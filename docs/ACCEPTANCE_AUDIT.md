## Completion decision

The hackathon alpha is delivered. The full goal is still active: actual Meet capture/sharing/performance, broader committed-answer accuracy, and end-to-end latency are not proved by the present evidence. Continue independent fixes and packaging; leave user-dependent live-call gates pending rather than claiming completion.
